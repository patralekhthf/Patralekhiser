# Myridius writing style profile

Captured 2026-08-19 from Satyam Patralekh. This is the source of truth the rule engine encodes.

## Audience

Readers in India, USA and the Philippines, including non native English speakers. Nobody should have to pause to decode a word or a sentence.

## Voice in one sentence

Clear enough for a customer, precise enough for a technologist, and concise enough for an executive.

## Characteristics

Direct without being aggressive. The point comes first, no buildup.

Concise but not artificially short. Enough context to make a decision, nothing that does not contribute.

Natural rather than polished to death. Reject language that sounds like corporate marketing, generic AI copy, or overly sophisticated prose.

Specific and concrete. "Track purchases, payments, and expenses" beats "Gain comprehensive visibility into financial operations."

Structured thinking. Problem, then capability, then business outcome, then action.

Business value focused. Features matter, but the language explains what they enable: control, speed, clarity, reduced risk, better decisions.

Confident but restrained. No "revolutionary", "game-changing", "ultimate" unless genuinely defensible.

Executive friendly. Senior leaders can scan and understand without decoding jargon.

Tactful. State facts and ownership clearly without assigning blame.

SEO conscious without SEO looking copy. Important phrases appear naturally, never mechanically repeated.

Simple language. No complex sentences unless required. Analogies and metaphors welcome.

Not minimalist for its own sake. Detail stays when it helps someone understand, evaluate or act. What gets cut is filler, not substance.

## Explicitly rejected

Em dashes and en dashes (and avoid hyphens where possible). Unnecessary contractions in formal writing. Emojis and decorative icons in professional documents. Colorful or gimmicky formatting. Words such as "delve". Repetitive headings. Excessive card style content. Copy that obviously feels AI generated.
