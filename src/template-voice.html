Style configuration

These rules define the Myridius voice. Edit them below, then press Apply. Use Export to save your configuration as a file and Import to load it back later. This page keeps nothing after you close it, so export after making changes.

Resemblance scoring

Each dimension scores 0 to 4 from how many flags appear per thousand words. Weight sets how much it contributes to the score out of 20. "No personal voice" is judged by what is absent, so it never highlights anything in the text.

Behaviour

Invisible characters

Zero-width spaces, bidi controls, tag characters, noncharacters and private-use codepoints are invisible on screen but defeat every rule below and travel into your exported files. They are always reported. Characters that are doing real work are kept and listed with the reason: emoji joiners, Persian and Devanagari joiners, complete flag sequences, and CJK, Mongolian, Khmer and Hangul selectors and fillers. Anything inside a code span is reported and left alone, so an article about invisible characters survives its own subject matter. Finding none is not evidence that a text was written by a person, and finding some is not evidence that it was not.

Space normalisation changes typography, not just bytes: a non-breaking space becoming an ordinary one lets 10 km, 10 % and Fig. 3 break across lines, and changes French spacing around guillemets. On by default because business prose rarely depends on it.

Folding lookalikes will damage genuine Cyrillic: real Russian containing а, е, о, р, с or х becomes Latin mojibake. Leave it off unless you know the document is English.

Protected terms

Never changed by any rule. One per line. Brand and product names belong here.

Blocked words and phrases (flag only)

Flagged for a human, never auto changed. Format: word | reason | suggestion | dimension | replacement. Dimension is d1 to d5; a replacement makes the flag acceptable in one click.

Simpler word swaps

Complex word to plain word. Format: find => replace, one per line. Add | verbOnly to a line when the word is also a noun with a different meaning, and the swap will only fire where the surrounding words make the verb sense certain. That is why harness the power of X becomes use while an eval harness is left alone.
